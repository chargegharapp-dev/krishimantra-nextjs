## 3. What We Can Help With

- **Account help:** registration, login problems, updating your details, and deleting your account.
- **Farming problems:** questions about a crop or animal problem you submitted, or a reply you have not yet received.
- **Free consultation:** soil and water quality guidance, animal care, and agriculture and farming advice.
- **Learning content:** courses that will not open, content errors, or topics you would like us to add.
- **Marketplace orders:** order status, cancellations, delivery problems, returns, replacements and refunds.
- **Sellers:** creating or editing listings, handling orders, and reporting problem buyers.
- **Reports:** unsafe or misleading listings, abusive messages, fake accounts or suspected fraud.
- **Privacy requests:** access to your data, correction, or deletion.
- **Feedback and suggestions** to improve the App.

## 4. How to Contact Us for the Fastest Help

Please include as much of the following as you can:

- Your **name and registered mobile number** (never your password).
- A short description of the problem and when it happened.
- For order problems: the **order number**, product name and the Seller’s name.
- **Photos or screenshots** if possible. For product problems, clear photos or a video of the product and packaging.
- The type and model of your phone, if the App is not working properly.

## 6. Marketplace Help


### Cancelling an order

Open **My Orders**, choose the order and tap **Cancel Order** before the Seller dispatches it. If the order is already dispatched, contact the Seller or us immediately. Because payment is Cash on Delivery, nothing is charged before delivery.


### Problem with a delivered product

- Report it within **24 hours** (fresh or perishable products) or **3 days** (seeds, grains, tools and other non-perishable products).
- Send clear photos or a video of the product and packaging, with your order number.
- We review the claim and help the Seller and Buyer agree on a replacement or a refund. Refunds are settled directly between Buyer and Seller, as Krishi Mantra does not process or hold any money.

### Reporting a listing or a user

Use the **Report** option in the App, or email us with the listing or user name and what is wrong. We review reports and may remove content, warn or suspend accounts.


## 7. Farming Emergencies

**Important:** Krishi Mantra consultation is general guidance and is not an emergency service. For a seriously sick animal, poisoning, or any health emergency, contact a local veterinarian, health post, or emergency service immediately. Do not wait for a reply from the App.


## 8. Stay Safe: Protect Your Account

- Krishi Mantra will **never ask for your password** by phone, message or email.
- We will **never ask for money, bank details or payment information.** The App has no online payment. Orders are paid only in cash on delivery.
- If someone claiming to be from Krishi Mantra asks for any of the above, do not respond and report it to support@krishimantra.app.
- Do not share one-time codes or your password with anyone, including other users.

## 9. If You Are Not Satisfied

- **Contact support** with the details of your problem, as above.
- **Escalate:** if you are not happy with the reply, write to support@krishimantra.app with the subject “Escalation” and the earlier reference or date. A senior team member will review it.
- **Further options:** if the issue is still not resolved, you may approach the relevant consumer or government authority in Nepal.
